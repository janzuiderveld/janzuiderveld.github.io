The pursuit of efficiency once replaced shopkeepers with vending machines. Today, laboratories at the forefront of organoid intelligence research are growing human brain cells to develop a more efficient alternative to silicon chips.

Vending Machine Organoid puts this research to work inside an everyday vending machine, replacing its processor with human brain organoids. Normally, the exchange is simple: insert a coin, enter a code, receive a product. Here, coins and keypresses stimulate living neural tissue, whose activity holds your credit and determines what is selected. To receive a product, you must adapt to its fatigue, fluctuations and rhythms.

Inside this machine, human brain cells work for coins.

Created in collaboration with Xinyi Zhang, supported by Finalspark, co-produced by V2_ Lab for the Unstable Media as part of the Microdosing A.I. art and technology residencies.
