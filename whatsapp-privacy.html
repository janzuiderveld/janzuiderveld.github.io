JAN ZUIDERVELD / CODEX MESSAGING GATEWAY

Privacy & data deletion

Last updated: 6 October 2026

I use Codex Messaging Gateway to help manage my WhatsApp correspondence: find messages, read conversations and prepare replies. It connects through Meta's official WhatsApp Business Platform. Replies require my explicit approval before they are sent.

This notice covers that integration, not all activity on my website or WhatsApp generally. It applies to messages handled by the integration; it does not mean that every message sent to me, or my entire phone history, is connected.

Who is responsible

Jan Zuiderveld, Netherlands, is responsible for the personal data processed through this integration. For privacy questions or requests, email jan@warana.xyz. You can contact me by email instead of using WhatsApp.

What is processed

The integration receives messages and delivery updates from Meta for a connected WhatsApp Business account. It keeps a local record of phone numbers, available profile names, message text, timestamps, message type and identifiers, reply references, and delivery or read status. It also stores reply drafts and the messages sent through it.

For some non-text messages, the record contains a caption, button response, location name or address, or a label indicating the message type. The current integration does not automatically download photo, video, document or audio files. It is not a complete copy of WhatsApp history. Older messages are available only if separately imported or synchronized through an explicitly enabled, supported process.

Why I use it

I use this information to keep track of correspondence, find relevant context, prepare replies and check whether messages were delivered. I rely on my legitimate interests in managing and securing my correspondence, balanced against the privacy of the people contacting me. Where a conversation is necessary to arrange or perform an agreement with you, that purpose can also provide the basis for processing. Records may be retained where a legal obligation requires it.

I do not sell message data or use this integration for advertising profiles. It does not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about people. AI can help read or draft text, but sending is subject to my approval. Please avoid sending sensitive information that is not needed for our conversation.

Where information goes

  • My computer: normalized message records and drafts are stored locally with access restricted to my operating-system account. Full incoming webhook payloads are not saved by the integration.
  • Meta / WhatsApp: supplies the messaging API and processes messages and delivery information as part of providing WhatsApp.
  • OpenAI: when I use Codex to read, search or work with messages, the relevant tool results and conversation content can be sent to OpenAI for AI processing and retained in the Codex conversation. Local message storage does not mean that all processing stays on my computer. OpenAI's processing, retention and use for service improvement depend on the service, account settings and applicable terms.
  • Cloudflare: the current webhook tunnel carries message and delivery data from Meta to my computer. Cloudflare processes that traffic as the network service provider.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts this notice. Opening it exposes normal web-request information, such as your IP address, to the hosting service. WhatsApp message records are not published on this website.

These providers may process information outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Their applicable terms and privacy notices describe their international-transfer arrangements and safeguards. Contact me for information about a particular processing arrangement.

Provider information: WhatsApp, OpenAI, Cloudflare and GitHub.

How long records remain

Records are kept according to their continuing relevance to our correspondence, any agreement or legal record-keeping requirement, and requests for deletion. The current local store has no automatic deletion schedule: records remain until manually removed. A reply draft can only be sent within 15 minutes of preparation; that expiry is not an automatic deletion of the draft file.

Copies in WhatsApp, Codex conversations or provider systems have their own retention and deletion controls. Removing a local message record does not automatically remove those other copies.

Deletion requests and your rights

Email jan@warana.xyz with the subject “WhatsApp data request”. Identify the WhatsApp number or conversation concerned and say whether you want access, correction or deletion. Do not send a password, verification code or identity document. I may ask for proportionate information to verify that the request is yours.

You can also request restriction of processing, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and request portability where applicable. I will respond within the applicable legal deadline, normally one month, and explain any lawful reason a request cannot be fully fulfilled. Deletion covers the integration records under my control and relevant copies I can remove through the services I use; it cannot erase a message from someone else's device or override a provider's legal retention duties.

You can complain to the Dutch supervisory authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, or your local data-protection authority.

This page and future changes

This notice uses no analytics scripts, advertising pixels, embedded services or cookies of its own. It is publicly accessible without logging in, even though it is not linked from the main navigation. I will update the date and text when the integration's data handling changes.
